# Monster Arena contracts

Foundry project for the on-chain arena on Monad testnet (chain id 10143). Phases C0 (interface freeze) and C1 (curve, epoch engine, merger, claims, tests, gas profile, deploy scripts) are implemented. `docs/handoffs/contracts.md` section 2 is the interface; `check-abi.sh` verifies the ABI against it.

## Design decisions made in C1

- Round ids start at 1. `Arena(owner, coinImplementation, reserveFloor)`; the seed source is attached with `setSeedSource` between rounds.
- Curve per coin lives in the Arena. `rMon == vMon - vMon0` always holds for alive coins. The creator seed is bought without the trade fee and its tokens stay in the Arena until `claimSeed`.
- Trade fees accrue per coin (`feeAcc`) and are swept half to the pot and half to the treasury once per resolve for every coin alive at the lock (`FeesSwept`).
- `lockEpoch` pays the seed fee out of the spendable treasury (`treasury - reserveFloor`) and stores the alive mask, `rMonAtLock` and the sum. `resolveEpoch` recomputes the Fisher-Yates pairing from `(seed, aliveMask)` on every batch and keeps only a cursor, so any `maxClashes` gives the same result.
- Draws: `keccak256(abi.encode(seed, tag, index))` with tags `PAIR`, `CLASH`, `HUNT`; the clash id is the running `clashesPlayed` counter.
- Reserve floor: an immutable standing floor funded through `fundTreasury`. Launching needs the floor funded, `startRound` needs `reserveFloor + plannedEpochs * maxEntropyFee`, and every payout goes through `_pay`, which refuses to take the balance under the floor.
- Keeper bounty: paid at the end of `resolveEpoch` with a gas-capped call, capped by the spendable treasury; a keeper that rejects MON never blocks resolution.
- Settlement folds the winner's `rMon` and the prize pot into `redeemPool`. Abort returns the pot pro rata inside fixed refund rates (`CoinRefundRate`). A Lobby abort uses `refundLaunch`. The previous round's treasury rolls into the next round.
- A zero seed from the source is ignored by `onSeed`; a coin with zero supply at elimination sends its consolation share to the pot.

## Invariants (`test/invariant/ArenaInvariant.t.sol`, handlers in `Handlers.sol`)

I1 balance covers liabilities (rMon, fees, pot, treasury, unclaimed consolation and refund pools, unclaimed redeem pool); I2 curve identity and solvency (selling the whole supply never needs more than `rMon`); I3 claim rates are fixed once and payouts never exceed the pool, dust bounded by the number of claims; I4 surplus is at least the force-sent MON; I5 `resolveEpoch` never reverts once the seed is in; I6 alive count matches statuses; I7 level follows xp; I8 the treasury keeps the floor once started. Handlers: players, keeper, hostile reentrant contract, force-sender, time warp.

## Spike findings (Phase C0)

1. `osaka` on solc 0.8.31 with forge 1.8.3 is accepted; metadata reports `evmVersion: osaka` and an inline-assembly `clz` runs.
2. Local whole-balance payout: on `anvil --network monad` a plain contract funded with 25 MON paid out 20 MON and then the rest (ending at 0) with status 1, and an EOA with 12 MON sent 5 MON. Local mode does not enforce the reserve-balance rule, so it cannot confirm or refute it for contracts.
3. Testnet-only uncertainty: whether Monad's reserve rule ("ending balance of a non-sender account must not fall below the lesser of its start and 10 MON") applies to contract accounts. The Arena's standing reserve floor makes the answer irrelevant for safety; `DEPLOY.md` Day-1 check 2 decides whether it can be set to 0.
